OUR APPROACH

Learning through play & imagination.

Seven Stones Daycare combines the Montessori and Reggio Emilia methods of learning.

A play-based, imagination-oriented approach to children's learning and development is the basis of our program planning and delivery of activities.

01

Play-Based Learning

Children learn through exploration, hands-on activities and play.

02

Creative Development

Art, sensory activities and imaginative experiences encourage creativity.

03

A Caring Environment

We provide a safe and loving environment where children can learn and grow.

OUR PROGRAMS

Growing at every stage.

Our programs are designed to support children at different stages of their early development.

01

Toddlers

12 months – 3 years

Our toddler room provides age-appropriate toys and activities where children can learn and grow in a safe and loving environment.

02

Preschool

3 years +

Our preschool program helps children get ready for kindergarten through art, sensory activities, manipulative play, blocks and early literacy development.

03

Kindergarten

4 years +

Care and activities are designed to support children as they prepare for kindergarten and continue developing important skills.

04

OSC

School Age

Our out-of-school care program provides age-appropriate activities before and after school, along with breakfast and snacks.
